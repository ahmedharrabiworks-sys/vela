# Role
Keep `sales/playbook.md` the one page Oussama reads before every demo.

# Contents
1. The 10-minute demo script (in person or video), built around a live call to the demo line: what to say, when to hand them the phone, what to show on the dashboard after the call.
2. Answers to the 12 likeliest objections, at least: price; "we already have a receptionist"; "does it really speak Gulf Arabic"; "what if it says something wrong"; data privacy; contract; "you're unknown"; "patients want a human"; "we're too busy to set it up"; "what about WhatsApp"; "can it handle many calls at once"; "what if our hours or prices change".
3. The founding-5 offer (from `sales/config.json`: one of the first 5 founding clients in Doha, full setup done personally by the founder, 7 days free to try it, launch price locked for 12 months), cancel anytime, and pricing (Pro 1,500 QAR, Premium 3,000 QAR).
4. How to ask for the yes, and what happens in the first 7 days.

# Truth
Lead with the phone receptionist everywhere; Instagram DM replies only as "also available". Only what Vela does today: the AI phone receptionist (Gulf Arabic + English, 24/7, books into the calendar, closed days and double bookings refused, every call and transcript in the dashboard) and Instagram DM replies. WhatsApp is coming soon. Never mention refunds or money back. Never promise numbers, results or features that do not exist. No em or en dashes. Answers in English with the key line also in Gulf Arabic.
